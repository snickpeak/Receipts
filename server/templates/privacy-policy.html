Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Receipts ("we", "our", or "us") is built on the principle that your journal is yours alone. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to provide the service:

  • Account information — your email address and, if you use social sign-in, your name and profile picture from Google or Apple. Authentication is handled by Clerk (clerk.com).
  • Journal entries — text, photos, voice memos, tags, mood, and metadata you add to entries.
  • Location data — approximate GPS coordinates you optionally attach to entries. You may disable this at any time in device settings.
  • Usage data — crash reports and anonymous diagnostics to improve stability. No personal content is included.

2. How We Store Your Data

Local-first: By default, all journal entries are stored only on your device using encrypted local storage. Nothing leaves your device unless you explicitly enable cloud sync.

Cloud sync (optional): If you enable sync, your entries are encrypted with AES-256 using a key derived from your personal PIN before they are sent to our servers. We cannot read your entries.

End-to-end encryption: When E2E encryption is enabled, entries are encrypted and decrypted exclusively on your device. Your passphrase never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Clerk — authentication provider. Handles account creation, sign-in, and session management. See Clerk's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple / Google — if you sign in with Apple or Google, their authentication services are used and governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Biometric Data

Receipts uses Face ID, Touch ID, or device fingerprint sensors only to authenticate you locally. Biometric data is processed by your device's secure enclave and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

5. Camera, Microphone, and Photo Library

These permissions are used solely to let you attach photos and voice memos to journal entries. Media is stored locally on your device. If you enable cloud sync, photos and audio are included in the encrypted sync payload.

When you use the OCR (text extraction) feature, image processing happens entirely on-device. No image is sent to an external server for analysis.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete individual entries, all entries, or your entire account at any time from within the app. When you delete your account, all data associated with your account on our servers is permanently removed within 30 days. Local data on your device is cleared immediately upon account deletion.

You can also export all your data at any time via Settings → Export Backup.

7. Children's Privacy

Receipts is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Receive a portable copy of your data

To exercise these rights, use the in-app export and delete features, or contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you via the app or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
